SUNDAE · display face · settled: 03 Bricolage Grotesque 800

Syne is out. Bricolage Grotesque 800 is in. The other six are kept here as the record.

Body stays Poppins and labels stay Space Mono — only the display face changes below. Each one is shown at hero size, at H2, and at H3, because a face that's great at 7rem can fall apart at 1.5rem. Tell me a number.

01Archivo BlackThe poster face

sundae

Why it fitsWide, heavy, flat-sided — this is closest to what 1980s Memphis posters actually used. Zero quirk, all mass. The color does the playing; the type just hits hard.
Watch forOnly one weight exists. No 700/900 range to modulate with, so hierarchy has to come from size alone.
